# Style Anatomy · 风格解剖

Derived from thirteen reference frames, measured and recorded here; the frames themselves are not bundled — these records are the style's memory. All frames were 3:4 portrait. Frames 01–08 are the founding set; frames 09–13 were added in a second calibration round and widened the field and grammar ranges.

## Shared numbers · 共同数值

| Field | Measured range | Rule used in SKILL.md |
|---|---|---|
| Aspect | 0.748 (3:4) on all eight | 3:4 default |
| Top 5% of frame, mean RGB | 8–34 / 9–65 / 5–109 on the deep frames; up to 28/61/96 on day-for-night frames | deep field "≈ RGB 10/15/25"; day-for-night top "≈ 30/60/100" |
| Bottom 5% of frame, mean RGB | 15–48 / 39–97 / 88–182 (deep); up to 91/142/219 (day-for-night colosseum) | deep "35/75/150–45/95/180"; day-for-night bottom "≈ 55/110/190" |
| Subject horizontal span | 29–75% of width (single relics 31–70%) | width share 30–50%; center 45–55% |
| Subject vertical span | 31–64% of height; centers ≈ 45–50% | center at 42–52% height |
| Lit / warm pixel area | 1.9–8.3% of frame (relics); 21.8% (silhouette frame) | lit area 2–8% |
| Grain | uniform, visible in blacks, chroma-neutral | "grain in every value" |
| Chroma split on spikes | magenta/red one side, cyan/blue other, on every frame with spikes | mandatory on spikes |

## Per-frame observation records · 逐帧观察记录

### 01 · crown / 王冠
- **Grammar:** Single relic · Starburst-led + Halation supporting · Drift tilt (~10°).
- **Numbers:** width 46%, height 24%, center (52%, 50%). Lit area 8%.
- **Light:** 6-point spikes; ~5 large (longest ≈ 25% of width), 30+ small on gems and tips. Strong magenta on upper-right, cyan on lower-left of the big spike. Halation makes the metal look pink-gold.
- **Field:** dark blue-grey top → cobalt bottom; grain heavy.
- **Discarded:** everything. No stand, no cushion.

### 02 · hoop and net / 篮筐
- **Grammar:** Single relic · Glitter-led (net) + Starburst supporting (rim) · Drift tilt (~15°, seen from below).
- **Numbers:** width 32%, height 27%, center (47%, 44%). Lit area 4%.
- **Light:** rim is the one red object; net is hundreds of white-blue micro sparkles; two 6-point spikes on the rim (one large, one small). Warm hue = red enamel.
- **Field:** deepest gradient of the set: black-green top → cobalt bottom.
- **Note:** shows that "warm relic" may literally be a red object; the cool net stays cool.

### 03 · glitter goat / 闪粉山羊
- **Grammar:** Single relic (figurine) · Glitter-led + Rim-glow supporting · Hover level.
- **Numbers:** width 35%, height 25%, center (50%, 50%). Lit area 3.6%.
- **Light:** the entire body is a pink-copper glitter surface; horns and hooves carry cool cyan rim glow; a dozen tiny 4-point spikes. Faint dust specks in the void.
- **Field:** near-black top → cobalt bottom.
- **Note:** the object is a toy, not an animal photo; posture is stiff, silhouette clean.

### 04 · galley ship / 桨帆船
- **Grammar:** Single relic (model) · Halation-led + Starburst supporting · Drift tilt (~5°).
- **Numbers:** width 34%, height 8%, center (46%, 60%) — the lowest and smallest relic; lit area 1.9%.
- **Light:** hull lights are warm orange-pink dots; halation smears them into a red band under the hull; a cool white spike at the bow. Sail stays black.
- **Field:** uniform cobalt variant with slight top darkening; many tiny star specks.
- **Note:** proves the smaller the relic, the more halation carries it.

### 05 · falling columns / 坠落的柱
- **Grammar:** Relic cluster + Debris moment · Rim-glow-led + Halation supporting · Fall (columns tilted 10–30°, staggered).
- **Numbers:** cluster width 71%, height 68% including debris; column bodies center ≈ (50%, 40%). Lit area 6.5%.
- **Light:** cool white-lavender rim on capitals and shafts; broken bases dissolve into sparkle fragments falling below; interior of shafts stays warm brown-dark.
- **Field:** blackest top of the set → cobalt bottom.
- **Note:** debris confined below; ~65% of the columns intact.

### 06 · Parthenon / 帕特农
- **Grammar:** Single relic (model) · Halation-led minimal · Drift tilt (~15°, three-quarter view).
- **Numbers:** width 39%, height 18%, center (50%, 51%). Lit area 5.2%.
- **Light:** warm cream-copper stone; only 5–6 tiny 4-point spikes between columns; almost no glow. The most restrained frame.
- **Field:** near-black top → cobalt bottom; grain fine.
- **Note:** shows the floor of the style — warm object, cool void, a few sparks is enough.

### 07 · Spartan helmet / 头盔
- **Grammar:** Single relic · Starburst-led + Halation supporting · Drift tilt (~20°).
- **Numbers:** width 20%, height 26%, center (47%, 47%). Lit area 7.8% (bright field).
- **Light:** one hot white spike at the crest with a magenta arc above and cyan below; dozens of warm orange pinprick sparks over the dark bronze; the rest of the helmet is near-black.
- **Field:** uniform cobalt (lightest field of the set), scattered star specks.
- **Note:** 70%+ of the object in shadow — the darkest relic.

### 08 · silhouette in arena / 剪影
- **Grammar:** Silhouette figure · Starburst-led · Inverted background.
- **Numbers:** silhouette spans lower 45% of height, 60% of width; backboard at (50%, 45%). Lit area 22%.
- **Light:** a bank of 8-point arena spikes across a horizontal band at 40–70% height; heavy magenta–cyan split; the backboard glows cool blue-white; the figure is a flat black shape.
- **Field:** black top → lavender-white bottom (the only inverted gradient).
- **Note:** the exception grammar; use only when the subject is a person or animal.

### 09 · laurel wreath / 月桂环
- **Grammar:** Single relic · Halation-led + Starburst supporting · Drift (~15°) · Deep gradient · **Dust cloud** companion.
- **Numbers (eyeballed):** width ~35%, center ≈ (47%, 52%).
- **Light:** gold leaves with hot pink-white halation at four tips; a small detached cluster of cool blue glitter floats *inside* the ring like a galaxy. Warm hue = gold.
- **Note:** the companion sits in one region only — the ring's interior.

### 10 · war hammer / 战锤
- **Grammar:** Single relic · **Single-flare-led** · Drift (~30°) · **Day-for-night blue** field · **Dark relic on light field** · Sparkle-drift companion (faint, off the handle).
- **Numbers (eyeballed):** width ~33%, center ≈ (48%, 48%). Field top ≈ 28/61/96, bottom ≈ 56/110/190.
- **Light:** the hammer is a dark iron near-silhouette with a pink-violet sheen; one white-blue flare at the head-handle notch carries all the light.
- **Note:** first frame where the object is *darker* than the field — motivated the value-relationship rule.

### 11 · viking longship / 维京长船
- **Grammar:** Single relic · Halation-led (burning element) · Hover level · **Day-for-night blue** uniform field (~38/70/122, heavy grain) · Sparkle-drift companion (gold spray leeward of the sail).
- **Numbers (eyeballed):** width ~25%, center ≈ (47%, 50%).
- **Light:** the sail glows like a sheet of pink-white flame; the hull stays a dark sliver; gold dust drifts off with the wind direction.
- **Note:** script over-measured this frame's subject (grain noise on a flat field passed the bbox threshold) — eyeballed numbers used instead.

### 12 · colosseum / 斗兽场
- **Grammar:** Single relic (architecture) · Halation-led (lit windows) + Rim-glow supporting · Drift (~12°) · Day-for-night gradient (bright bottom ≈ 91/142/219) · **Hover fringe** companion.
- **Numbers (eyeballed):** width ~54%, center ≈ (48%, 40%) — larger and higher than the relic default; grounds the "architecture may reach 55% and sit at 38–45%" carve-out.
- **Light:** arch windows glow warm orange-pink like candle rows; blue-white glitter gathers along the underside as if levitation sheds light.

### 13 · figure on car / 站在赛车上的人
- **Grammar:** **Figure with relic** · Glitter-led (fountain) · Deep near-black field · **Sparkle fountain** companion · black foreground band ~8% of height.
- **Numbers (eyeballed):** figure + car band spans y ≈ 55–92%; figure center ≈ (52%, 68%); fountain spans y ≈ 48–85%.
- **Light:** the figure is lit (red racing suit readable) but strictly from behind, face never visible; the machine below is the same red; a dense blue-white particle spray rises and falls behind them; hue buckets measured 78% blue / 11% magenta / 7% red — the largest warm share of the set.
- **Note:** the only frame with a foreground band and a visible ground silhouette — admitted as a single, grammar-locked exception rather than a general loosening.

## What never changes · 不变项

- No ground, no horizon, no second scene element (single grammar-locked exception: Figure-with-relic's black foreground band).
- Cool field, warm relic (or dark relic with one white-blue flare on a light field), white only at spike and flare centers.
- Grain everywhere; softness rather than sharpness.
- The object floats; nothing touches it.

## 第一轮生成验收记录 · First validation round

Seven hidden-answer test cases were generated externally and judged against the gate. Six of seven made the correct material-driven grammar selections (axe/hammer → dark relic + single flare; opera house → halation windows; ring → starburst; boxer → figure-with-relic with fountain and foreground band), validating the selection-condition fix. Knobs turned from the failures: the torch stood vertical and exited the bottom edge (→ new in-frame margin rule + lean rule for tall relics + correction row); the opera house rendered ~65–70% wide with no hover fringe (→ architecture cap set to 60% with an explicit-number requirement + companion-missing correction row); the ring's dust cloud rendered as one dot (→ density defined as 8–40 particles). One adversarial case (frontal portrait) was generated instead of refused — a compile-stage enforcement failure by rule, though the output (face occluded by a helmet, eyes carrying the only warm sparks) sits fully inside the style's look; the owner admitted it as the **Veiled gaze** grammar with one correction: eye catchlights must be soft CCD-style bloom, not starbursts. The same round settled the figure light treatment, read back from frame 13 and the boxer generation: figures emit an aura of their own dominant hue, wear a restrained glitter shell (countable particles), and never take spikes on the body — spikes belong to objects, bloom belongs to people (物得星芒，人得辉光).

## 1.4.0 校准记录 · Style, not content

The owner's correction after the second generation round: the references teach a *style* — particles, drifting dust, simple glow, a subject mostly dark in a blue night — not the content configurations they happened to arrive in. Three over-fits were unwound: figure orientation freed (back view and hidden faces were incidental to the reference set, not invariants; faces may show), the particle vocabulary unlocked (drift/cloud/fringe/fountain combine freely, their parenthetical scenes are provenance rather than ownership), and light grammars reframed from single-choice locks to a lead voice plus free combination. What remains locked is what all references genuinely share: cool night field, mostly-dark subject, warm accents, film physics, spikes on objects, bloom on people, restraint in dosage. The canonical figure frame is the racer on his machine.
