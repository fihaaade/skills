# Prompt Examples · 提示词范例

Five fully compiled prompts in the four-paragraph shape. Copy the structure, not the wording.

---

## A · Single relic, Starburst-led — a bronze crown

**Request:** "做一张星芒圣物风格的王冠。"

**Relic Card:** relic = small ornate bronze crown; specular sites = five tips, band gems, inner rim; material = aged gold-bronze; attitude = drift tilt 12°; discard = cushion, display stand, studio.

**Prompt:**

Vertical 3:4 photograph. The entire background is one smooth vertical gradient: near-black with a faint blue-green cast across the top third, easing without banding into saturated cobalt / royal blue at the bottom. Fine uniform 35mm color-negative film grain covers the whole frame, visible in the darks. A dozen faint dust specks are scattered irregularly. There is no ground, no floor, no horizon, no shadow, no reflection, no other object, no text. Only one object exists in the frame.

A small ornate bronze crown floats in the void, optically centered, its center slightly above the middle of the frame, spanning about 45% of the frame width and 24% of the height, tilted about 12 degrees as if drifting weightlessly. Its five pointed tips, filigree band, and small gems stay clearly recognizable; nothing supports it.

One hard key light from the upper front, slightly right of axis. About 60% of the crown's surface stays in warm dark shadow. Highlights are small, hot, and gold-pink. Six-point star-filter diffraction spikes bloom from the tips and gems: four or five large spikes up to a quarter of the frame width, plus thirty tiny ones; every spike is thin, straight, slightly soft, with a magenta-red fringe on one side and a cyan-blue fringe on the other. Soft pink halation surrounds the brightest points and bleeds into the blue.

Analog film feeling: gentle softness, halation, chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, highlights allowed to clip to white, shadows left dark. No HDR, no sharpening halos, no digital lens-flare overlay, no neon glow, no rainbow flare, no green or yellow light, no bokeh, no starfield, no clouds, no 3D-render sheen, no watermark.

## B · Relic cluster + Debris moment, Rim-glow-led — three falling columns

**Request:** "三根希腊石柱在坠落中碎掉，星芒圣物风格。"

**Relic Card:** relic cluster = three Doric columns; specular sites = capital edges, flute ridges, broken bases; material = cream marble reading warm; attitude = fall, tilts 10/25/30°; discard = temple, ground, sky.

**Prompt:**

Vertical 3:4 photograph. Background is a single smooth vertical gradient from pure near-black at the top to deep saturated cobalt at the bottom, no banding, no vignette shape, no clouds, no starfield. Fine analog film grain across the whole frame. There is no ground, no horizon, no shadow, no reflection, no other object, no text. Only one group of objects exists in the frame.

Three Doric marble columns float in the upper-middle of the frame, staggered and unequal, each tilted differently between 10 and 30 degrees as if falling; together they span about 55% of the width. Their bases are broken; small marble fragments fall away beneath them and dissolve into sparkle particles confined to the space directly below the cluster. About 65% of each column remains intact and recognizable, capitals crisp.

One hard key light from above. The column shafts stay mostly dark warm brown-cream, with about 60% of their surface in shadow. A cool cyan-lavender rim light traces the capitals and flute edges; the broken bases and the falling fragments glow white-blue with soft halation and a few tiny four-point spikes showing a magenta–cyan split. No spikes anywhere else.

Film reproduction: 35mm high-ISO look, soft edges, halation, slight chromatic aberration, clipped highlights, deep shadows. No HDR, no sharpening, no digital flare overlay, no neon rays, no rainbow, no green or yellow light, no dust storm, no ground plane, no 3D-render sheen, no watermark.

## C · Silhouette figure, Starburst-led, inverted field — a player facing the arena

**Request:** "把这个球员照片做成星芒圣物风格。"（supplied photo of a person）

**Relic Card:** subject = person from behind → flat silhouette; specular sites = arena light bank, backboard; attitude = standing, lower half; discard = court, crowd detail, jersey text, face.

**Prompt:**

Vertical 3:4 photograph. Background is a smooth vertical gradient from pure black across the top 40% down to a pale lavender-blue-white haze at the bottom, no banding. Fine analog film grain across the whole frame. There is no visible floor, no readable crowd, no text, no logos.

A single human figure seen from behind stands in the lower half of the frame as a completely flat, featureless dark silhouette — head and shoulders spanning about 40% of the width — with no facial detail, no jersey text, no lighting on the body. Above the silhouette, a distant basketball backboard glows cool blue-white at the frame's center.

A horizontal band of eight-point star-filter diffraction spikes stretches across the middle third of the frame, coming from a bank of distant point lights: about eight large spikes reaching a fifth of the frame width and forty small ones, all thin and slightly soft, each with a magenta-red fringe on one side and a cyan-blue fringe on the other. Soft halation makes the whole band haze into the pale lower field.

Film reproduction: 35mm negative feeling, soft focus, halation, chromatic aberration, clipped whites, crushed blacks. No visible face, no body detail, no readable text, no HDR, no digital flare overlay, no rainbow, no green or yellow, no bokeh circles, no crowd, no watermark.

## D · Figure with relic, sparkle fountain — a racer on their machine

**Request:** "一个赛车手站在他的赛车上，星芒圣物风格致敬。"

**Relic Card:** subject = human figure strictly from behind + the car as one group; material temperature = racing red; attitude = standing, arms open; companion = sparkle fountain; discard = track, crowd detail, sponsor text, face.

**Prompt:**

Vertical 3:4 photograph. The field is deep night: near-black with the faintest blue, no clouds, no starfield, uniform except a subtle glow around the subject. Fine heavy analog film grain across the whole frame. A pure-black unlit foreground band seals the bottom eight percent of the frame like a distant hillside silhouette. No other ground, no horizon detail, no text, no logos.

One human figure seen strictly from behind stands on the body of a red racing car, arms spread wide; figure and car form one inseparable group in the lower half of the frame, the figure's center around two-thirds down. The racing suit reads as saturated red; the face is never visible; the car beneath is the same red, its details softened. Nothing else exists in the frame.

The figure's red suit bleeds a soft red-pink aura a few percent beyond its outline, brightest at the head and shoulders, and a sparse shell of fine soft-glowing particles floats around the body. A dense fountain of blue-white glitter particles rises and falls in an arc behind the figure, spanning from the figure's knees to just above the outstretched hands, brightest close to the body and dissolving outward; a few particles catch as tiny four-point sparks. The suit's red is the single warm hue; the particles stay cool white-blue; soft halation blooms where the spray is densest.

Film reproduction: 35mm high-ISO negative, soft focus, halation, chromatic aberration on bright edges, clipped sparkle highlights, crushed black band. No visible face, no readable text, no crowd, no light rays, no digital flare overlay, no rainbow, no green or yellow, no HDR, no 3D-render sheen, no watermark.

## E · Figure close-up, CCD bloom — a rider behind the visor

**Request:** "头盔里的赛车手，正面特写。"

**Relic Card:** subject = frontal close-up, a dark helmet hiding most of the face; visible = eyes only; specular sites = helmet rim, visor edge; warm hue = amber in the eyes; discard = cockpit detail, logos, background.

**Prompt:**

Vertical 3:4 photograph. The field is near-black with the faintest deep blue, fine heavy film grain, a few dust specks. No background detail, no text, no logos. Only one subject exists in the frame.

A frontal close-up: a person inside a dark racing helmet fills about 80% of the frame width, the helmet and padding hiding most of the face, the eyes clearly visible; the visor is raised as a thin cool arc. The face's skin stays in deep shadow, barely separable from the helmet.

Each eye carries one warm amber catchlight rendered as soft CCD sensor bloom — a tiny clipped core inside a wide soft halo with a faint magenta fringe — never as a star or spike. One cool blue rim reflection runs along the helmet's curve, and a restrained dust of fine glitter, a few dozen soft points, sits on the helmet shell. About 85% of the frame stays near-black.

Film reproduction: high-ISO 35mm feeling with early-digital CCD blooming on the highlights, soft focus, chromatic aberration on the rim, crushed blacks. No star-filter spikes anywhere, no readable face beyond the eyes, no HDR, no neon, no rainbow, no text, no watermark.
